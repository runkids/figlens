# Change tracking

`index track` and `index untrack` (`src/workspace/jobs.rs`) record a tracked scope's revisions as it is published, compared with the shared [diff](diff.md) engine (#92). Generations, jobs and publication fences are in [index](index-jobs.md).

## Tracking

A build of a tracked scope (a `tracking` row not `disabled`) also writes its records' diff facets and the generation's facet layout and selected-node context (`facet_sets`), in the same fenced batches as records; they are deleted with their generation. An untracked build writes no facets. `index track` (CLI `index track --scope N [--source S]`, MCP `index` action `track`) needs the scope published: it sets the row `baseline_pending`, bumps its `epoch` and submits a `rebuild` of that scope, so the baseline never reuses an unchanged snapshot; a scope already tracked keeps its epoch and builds nothing unless its baseline is still pending, which builds it again. Only a rebuild that reads tracking on can record the baseline: one not yet past its `hash` stage (the worker reads tracking at parse), or one submitted after the pending baseline was enabled (`created_ms >= enabled_ms`), which a repeated `track` joins. While any other build of the scope runs (an older rebuild, or a refresh, which may reuse an unfaceted snapshot), `track` is refused `workspace_busy` before it changes anything, with a wait on that job and then `track` again: joining it would publish a generation without facets and leave the baseline pending. `index untrack` sets `disabled` and bumps the epoch; stored revisions stay. Removing a source deletes its tracking and revisions with it; a relocate keeps the id and the history.

## Revisions

Before that transaction, an actively tracked scope's previous and new generations are compared from the database in one read transaction (`diff::revision`, the same `compare` as `figlens diff`), outside any write. Inside it, the revision is written only if the `tracking` row is as read (same state and `epoch`) and the new generation has facets; otherwise the generation still publishes and no revision is written. `baseline_pending` writes a `baseline` revision (`reason: tracking_started`) and becomes `active`. A previous generation without facets, or with another `facet_version`, is a `baseline` too (`previous_untracked`, `facet_version_changed`), never compared, and so is a scope published again after eviction left it no published generation (`previous_unpublished`): never a silent gap. A comparison that fails (unreadable facets, I/O) still publishes, with a `changes` revision whose coverage is partial with `comparison_failed` and detail `truncated`, never an empty one. A revision stores exact counts per change kind and facet in `summary` and at most 10,000 change rows or 4 MiB (`detail: truncated` past either, or when the rows would pass `DISK_CAP`). A workspace-wide `revision_seq` in `meta` orders revisions, never the clock; ids are 16 random bytes. Retention works on whole groups in the same transaction: past 1,000 revisions per source and scope the oldest are deleted, and past 16 MiB of change rows the oldest keep only their summary (`detail: expired`). The replaced generation's facets are dropped once its revision commits.

## Reading changes

`changes` (`src/workspace/changes.rs`; CLI `figlens changes <select>`, MCP `changes`, read-only) never writes or builds. It reads one SQLite read transaction over the visible sources only (a `sourceId` filter must be visible; hidden sources are neither listed nor counted), optionally one `scope`. A poll pages revision groups oldest first (limit 1-50, default 20, at most 256 KiB) after `cursor`, else after `consumer`'s checkpoint, else from the oldest kept, and lists `tracking` (each tracked scope's `state`, `lastRevisionId`, `lastCheckedAtMs` and `refreshRequired` from the file's stamp against its published snapshot) apart from `monitor` (the watch monitor, `inactive` until B2). `revision` pages that revision's stored rows instead (limit 1-500, default 100), with `detail` explicit. A workspace before schema 4 has nothing recorded.

Revision ids are the 16 random bytes in hex. A cursor is `c1.` (poll) or `d1.` (detail: id and row) and the payload plus the first 16 bytes of an HMAC-SHA256 keyed by `meta.cursor_secret` over the incarnation, the filters and the hash of the visible source ids, all in hex: another workspace, incarnation, filter or visibility is `cursor_stale`, and the internal `seq` is never exposed. Retention records the newest deleted `seq` per source and scope in `meta` (`pruned`, source, scope). A poll cursor whose revision is gone, or a checkpoint behind that mark, is `event_gap`, whose recovery reads from the oldest cursor (a zero id: after the mark). Removing a source deletes its `pruned` marks and the checkpoints of filters naming it.

`watch ack` (CLI `figlens watch ack <select> --consumer C --cursor X`, MCP `watch` action `ack`) validates the cursor for the same filters and stores `consumers.after_seq` only forward, in one IMMEDIATE transaction, so a retry or an older cursor changes nothing (`advanced: false`); the reply carries a cursor at the checkpoint held.
