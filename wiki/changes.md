# Change tracking

`index track` and `index untrack` (`src/workspace/jobs.rs`) record a tracked scope's revisions as it is published, compared with the shared [diff](diff.md) engine (#92). Generations, jobs and publication fences are in [index](index-jobs.md).

## Tracking

A build of a tracked scope (a `tracking` row not `disabled`) also writes its records' diff facets and the generation's facet layout and selected-node context (`facet_sets`), in the same fenced batches as records; they are deleted with their generation. An untracked build writes no facets. `index track` (CLI `index track --scope N [--source S]`, MCP `index` action `track`) needs the scope published: it sets the row `baseline_pending`, bumps its `epoch` and submits a `rebuild` of that scope, so the baseline never reuses an unchanged snapshot; a scope already tracked keeps its epoch and builds nothing unless its baseline is still pending (a build that started before tracking stored no facets). `index untrack` sets `disabled` and bumps the epoch; stored revisions stay. Removing a source deletes its tracking and revisions with it; a relocate keeps the id and the history.

## Revisions

Before that transaction, an actively tracked scope's previous and new generations are compared from the database in one read transaction (`diff::revision`, the same `compare` as `figlens diff`), outside any write. Inside it, the revision is written only if the `tracking` row is as read (same state and `epoch`) and the new generation has facets; otherwise the generation still publishes and no revision is written. `baseline_pending` writes a `baseline` revision (`reason: tracking_started`) and becomes `active`. A previous generation without facets, or with another `facet_version`, is a `baseline` too (`previous_untracked`, `facet_version_changed`), never compared. A comparison that fails (unreadable facets, I/O) still publishes, with a `changes` revision whose coverage is partial with `comparison_failed` and detail `truncated`, never an empty one. A revision stores exact counts per change kind and facet in `summary` and at most 10,000 change rows or 4 MiB (`detail: truncated` past either, or when the rows would pass `DISK_CAP`). A workspace-wide `revision_seq` in `meta` orders revisions, never the clock; ids are 16 random bytes. Retention works on whole groups in the same transaction: past 1,000 revisions per source and scope the oldest are deleted, and past 16 MiB of change rows the oldest keep only their summary (`detail: expired`). The replaced generation's facets are dropped once its revision commits.
